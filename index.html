Who is Keanu Reeves

Kind, Respectful, Talented,

Keanu Charles Reeves was born September 2nd, 1964 in Beirut, Lebanon. He is a Canadian/American actor who started acting at age 9 but didnt make his professinal on-screen debut unitl 1984 on an episode of Hangin' In. Reeves didnt really pop up on critics' radars until his performance in the dark adolescent drama, Rivers Edge(1986). After which he landed a supporting role in the Oscar-nominated Dangerous Liaisons(1988) with director Stephen Frears. Keanu's first popular movie role was Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventur(1989). This role turned out to be a blessing and a curse for him though because although he was easily recognized as Ted, it tooks years for him to shake the Ted stigma everyone saw in him. His big-budget break wouldn't come until 5 years later with the release of Speed(1994). This success heralded an era of 5 years in which Reeves would alternate between a bunch of small films like Feeling Minnesota, The Devils Advacote and several more. Reeves then shocked everyone by passing up on the Speed sequal, but then Hit the box-office big time with the Wachowski siblings' cyberadventure, The Matrix(1999), which is a huge personal favoite of mine. After this he became a bonafide box-office star and would appear in dozens of films, including continueing the Matrix sage and turning it into a trilogy(The Matrix Reloaded, The Matrix Revolutions BOTHreleased in2003). Reeves has a great career with over 100 movies to his name including reprising roles in the Matrix series with The Matrix Resurrections(2021) and even more supriseing he role as Ted in Bill & Ted Face the Music(2020), 31 years after the release of the original. He even went on to launch another highly successful franchise the action packed John Whick series, which has 4 moives so far and more planned for the future.
